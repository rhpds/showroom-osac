= Introduction to Red Hat Cloud Extensions

Welcome to the Introduction to Red Hat Cloud Extensions workshop!

You're a Platform Engineer at Sovereign Cloud Corp, a European government services provider. Leadership has handed you a critical mission: deploy a sovereign cloud platform on OpenShift that meets strict data sovereignty and regulatory compliance requirements. The platform must support multiple government tenants, automate workload provisioning through a service catalog, and integrate with Keycloak for identity management.

This isn't just another cloud deployment. Your government clients need to know their data never leaves their jurisdiction, and every workload must be provisioned through an auditable, compliant process. You have the tools — and this workshop shows you how to use them.

== What you'll learn

In this workshop, you will:

* Navigate and understand the Red Hat Cloud Extensions platform architecture and components
* Create and manage multi-tenant environments with Ceph storage
* Use the osac CLI to create catalog items and deploy workloads
* Verify platform health and tenant readiness

== Who this is for

This workshop is designed for Platform Engineers and Cloud Architects who want to deploy and manage a complete Red Hat Cloud Extensions platform, including multi-tenant environments and automated workload provisioning.

**Experience level**: Intermediate

== Prerequisites

Before starting this workshop, you should have:

* Familiarity with OpenShift or Kubernetes concepts (pods, namespaces, operators)
* Basic command-line experience with `oc` or `kubectl`
* Understanding of multi-tenancy and role-based access control (RBAC)
* (Optional) Familiarity with Ceph storage concepts
